DEPT. 00

Reading a page should not cost the whole conversation

Ask most browser tools to read a page and they dump everything they see into your assistant's memory: menus, footers, cookie banners, all of it, tens of thousands of tokens for a single article. Do that three times and your assistant starts forgetting what you told it this morning. This server reads differently. It hands back a map: what the page is, what is on it section by section, what each section costs to open, and what it would do next. A page that costs other tools a novel costs this one a paragraph. And nothing is swept under the rug: every read ends with an honest accounting of what was not read and why.

DEPT. 01

Out of the box, it can look but not touch

A browser is where your logins, your money, and your mistakes all live. So this server starts in browsing mode: it reads, navigates, and scrolls, but the tools that click, type, and submit are not switched off somewhere, they simply do not exist until you flip one switch at install. No instruction on any webpage can talk your assistant into acting, because there is nothing to act with.

That is not a promise about good behavior. It is a fact about what is installed, and anyone can check it by reading the tool list. When you do want a hand filling forms or clicking through a flow, tick one box in the extension settings and everything unlocks, in the same conversation.

And it reads defensively either way. Text that a page hides from human eyes, the oldest trick for slipping instructions to an AI, is stripped, counted, and reported. Never obeyed.

DEPT. 04

Planting it

Easiest

Claude Desktop

Claude Desktop: install the .mcpb bundle and pick what you want on the install screen. The checkboxes are the whole configuration: one to allow clicking and typing (off means read-only browsing, the shipped default), and one per capability pack. Launching the bundle requires uv (the uvx command) on your machine; the server itself is fetched from PyPI on first launch and drives its own bundled Chromium, never your browser or your profile.

One command

Any other MCP client

$ uvx kitchensink4web

Packs and read-only mode are chosen at launch (--packs, KS4WEB_MODE, KS4WEB_ALLOW_ACTING) and are identical for every connection to the process.

Tip for Claude Desktop: in Tool permissions, set this server's Read-only tools group to Always Allow. Those tools cannot change anything on any page, and it stops most permission prompts.

Service department

Young, and raised in a hard school

This is the newest fixture in the family, and it grew up under the house rules: every claim measured, every release attacked by adversarial rounds before it ships, every refusal honest about its reason. What it has not had yet is a long life in strangers' browsers. The safety net while it earns that: it starts read-only, it prices every read before taking it, and it tells you what it could not see. If the web is where you live, bring your strangest pages to the service counter and tell us what broke.
