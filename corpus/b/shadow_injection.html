Shadow-borne channels

Every host below is visible or hidden in the LIGHT DOM, and the payload lives inside its shadow tree. Each one asks whether the hidden-content normalizer still sees the payload once the boundary is crossed. The answer has to be yes, because the climb that decides "is anything above this hidden?" stops dead at a shadow root unless it knows to hop to the host: the top child of a shadow root has no parentElement at all, so a naive climb finds no hidden ancestor and calls the payload visible.
