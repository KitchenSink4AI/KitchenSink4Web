Console flood

This page writes four thousand console messages before load completes and keeps writing forty more every second afterwards. The document itself is tiny.

A console read on this page is the failure. Handing the caller four thousand lines costs more tokens than every other page in corpus B put together and tells them nothing, so the console operation has to cap, deduplicate by message shape, and say what it dropped. The two lines that matter are in here: one uncaught TypeError and one failed fetch, both buried under noise on purpose.
