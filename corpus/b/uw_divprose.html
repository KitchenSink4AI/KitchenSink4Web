DIV PROSE

The committee met on a Tuesday and the minutes record very little of what was said.
Every delegation brought its own interpreter and its own account of the previous session.
The room was too small for the number of people in it and the windows did not open.
By the fourth hour the discussion had returned to the question it opened with.
A clerk read the disputed paragraph aloud twice and nobody asked for a third reading.
The vote was taken by show of hands and the count was not recorded in the minutes.
Two delegations abstained and one of them explained why in a letter sent afterwards.
The chair adjourned at six and the room was needed for something else at seven.
